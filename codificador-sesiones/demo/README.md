# Demo study (100 percent fictional data)

A ready-made research folder to learn, test and demo the agent **without touching any real team or
study**. Every person, client, study and quote here is invented; every Markdown document
carries a fictional-document notice ("DOCUMENTO FICTICIO"; the CSV and JSON files carry none). A privacy grep of this folder must return no real
person, company or client.

## What is inside

`es/investigacion/`, copied to Drive as `Investigación (demo)`: the research folder of **Brújula**,
a fictional four-person research team (Lucía is the lead, Sara and Andrés researchers, Tomás
consults).

- `_config/`: team with the Fathom rule, the output format derived from their quick-download
  spreadsheet (kept untouched in `_config/plantillas/`), and quality criteria with one good and one
  bad code.
- `_ejemplos/movilidad-2025/`: a previous study coded by hand (codebook, one focus-group transcript,
  the hand-made matrix with 7 rows). It is the calibration example and the fidelity test.
- `estudios/ocio-familias-2026/`: the current study, "Tiempo libre en familia 2026", with two target
  groups (G1 small children, G2 teenagers), a three-variable codebook (activities, who decides, main
  barrier), one G1 focus group already coded (8 rows, one passage left uncategorized), the matrix and
  the watermark.

**One focus group is not yet coded**, waiting in `estudios/ocio-familias-2026/sesiones/entrada/`
(G2, four participants). It contains one clear code per variable, a passage about outdoor recreation
that, with the uncategorized one from G1, reaches two participants and becomes a proposal, a turn
with no speaker, an unclear quote, a participant who names no barrier, and noise. Coding it is the
demo's before and after.

## How to use it

Ask: **"puebla el codificador con el estudio de ejemplo"**. Then:

1. "codifica el transcript que está en entrada": the G2 session file, its rows in the matrix, the
   proposal, the watermark.
2. "acepta la propuesta de recreación al aire libre": the codebook gains an accepted emergent
   category with its history line, and the two passages become rows.
3. "cruza actividades de tiempo libre con quién decide por grupo": step 1 and the pause. With three
   and four participants per group, most candidates stay under the minimum: that is the point.
4. "¿qué dijo la gente del grupo 2 sobre el dinero?": the count and the quotes.
5. "corre la prueba de fidelidad": the comparison against the hand-made matrix.
6. Ask again to code what is in the inbox: the session is already coded and nothing changes.
