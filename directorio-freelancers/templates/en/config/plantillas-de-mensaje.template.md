# Message templates

How <Organization> writes to its freelancers. The request drafter builds every message from
this, in the channel and language of each person's card. Every message is a draft: a person
pastes and sends it from their phone.

<!-- Current since YYYY-MM-DD (origin: installation interview) -->

## What a message always carries

1. A greeting with the person's name.
2. Date and time of the shoot, and estimated duration.
3. Place (or city and kind of place if the exact address comes later).
4. What will be done and which role is needed.
5. The requirement, if any ("a valid safety course is required").
6. Until when an answer is expected (the job type's window).
7. Who signs.

## Example (English, WhatsApp)

> Hi <Name>, are you available on <day> <date> from <time> to <time> for <job> at <place>?
> A valid <certification> is required. Could you confirm before <deadline>? Thanks,
> <who signs>.
