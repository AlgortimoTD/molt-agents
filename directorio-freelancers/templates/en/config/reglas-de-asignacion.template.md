# Assignment rules

What the team keeps in its head about whom to call for what, written down. The installation
interview fills it and the coordinator corrects it when it changes. The request drafter
reads this file before building any shortlist; what is not here is not applied.

## Job types

One type per row. "Principals" are the people who get asked first, in order; "preferred" is
the first of that list. The mode says how to ask: `sequential` (the preferred person first
and, if they do not answer within the window, the next one) or `simultaneous` (all the
principals at once; the first to answer gets it).

| Job type | Principals (in order) | Mode | Wait window | Required certification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <construction site photography> | <Name 1>, <Name 2>, <Name 3> | sequential | <2 hours> | <construction safety course> |
| <event video> | <Name 1>, <Name 2> | simultaneous | <not applicable> | <none> |
| <short-deadline editing> | <Name 1> | sequential | <1 hour> | <none> |

A person who is not in the row of a type can still enter the shortlist of that type if their
card says they do that work; they go after the principals and the reason says so.

## What takes someone out of the shortlist

- A required certification expired or undated on the date of the shoot. Always.
- An assignment limit on their card that clashes with the job (for example "no
  short-deadline editing" for a one-day edit).
- Status `paused` or `do not call` on their card.
