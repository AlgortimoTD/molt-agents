# Message templates

<!-- FICTIONAL DOCUMENT: invented production company for the directorio-freelancers plugin demo. No person, company or client is real. -->

How North Beacon writes to its freelancers. The request drafter builds every message from
this, in the channel and language of each person's card. Every message is a draft: a person
pastes and sends it from their phone.

<!-- Current since 2026-09-15 (origin: installation interview) -->

## What a message always carries

1. A greeting with the person's name.
2. Date and time of the shoot, and estimated duration.
3. Place (or city and kind of place if the exact address comes later).
4. What will be done and which role is needed.
5. The requirement, if any ("a valid construction safety course is required").
6. Until when an answer is expected (the job type's window).
7. Who signs: Lucía.

## Tone per channel

| Channel | Tone | Length |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp | close, informal, no formalities | four to six lines |
| Messenger | just as close; no long links | four to six lines |

## Tone per language

| Language | Notes |
|---|---|
| English | first name; short sentences; "safety course" for the construction safety course, "heights course" for the heights course |
| Spanish | tuteo; nombre de pila; "curso de seguridad en obra" for the construction safety course, "curso de trabajo en alturas" for the heights course |

## Example (English, WhatsApp)

> Hi Bel, are you available tomorrow, Tuesday October 7, from 7:00 to 11:00 to photograph
> the beams of the new bridge on the north road? A valid safety course is required. Could
> you confirm before 9:00 today? Thanks, Lucía.
