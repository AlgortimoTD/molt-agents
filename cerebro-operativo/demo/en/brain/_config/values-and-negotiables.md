# Values, negotiables and non-negotiables

This file decides how the brain answers when a client is involved, and it is the only one
**Marina** edits directly. Every change the routine proposes waits for her approval; Tomás
executes what is approved.

## Values

In the team's words (interview 2026-09-01):

- **The team comes first.** "A client who mistreats the team costs us more than they pay."
  (Marina)
- **Say what is going to happen before it happens.** "If a delivery is going to slip, the
  client knows it the day we know it, not on the delivery day." (Tomás)
- **Work we can show.** "We do not take projects we would not put in our portfolio."
  (Marina)

## Negotiables

- **The scope of a piece**, as long as the price adjusts with it. Julia decides it with Tomás
  (interview 2026-09-01).
- **Pending confirmation:** deadlines, payment terms and rounds of changes. Marina agreed to
  complete the list (`memory.md`, 2026-09-01).

## Non-negotiables

- **How the team is treated.** No client writes to the team outside the agreed channel and
  hours; if it happens, Marina takes over the conversation. It was born from the Nébula case
  (`cases/2025-nebula.md`).
- **Nothing goes out without Marina's review** if it carries the client's brand outward
  (interview 2026-09-01).

## When a client is let go, and how it is said

When a non-negotiable is broken for the second time after it was put in writing. Marina
decides it, it is said on a call (never by message), the team offers to close the open
deliverables and hands over all of the client's material. Source: interview 2026-09-01 and
the Nébula case.
