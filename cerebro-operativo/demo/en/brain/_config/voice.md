# Faro Estudio's voice

Generated with the `brand-voice-style-guide` skill on 2026-09-01, from Faro's public site
(home and "about us") and three emails from Marina to clients that the team handed over.

**Missing, and it shows:** the social media posts, which an external freelancer writes. This
file describes the voice Faro uses to write to its clients, not necessarily the voice of its
social media.

**Language:** English throughout.

---

## In one line

Faro writes the way Marina talks in a meeting with a client: direct, warm, no frills, and
always saying what happens next.

## Tone axes

| Axis | Where it sits | Real example |
|---|---|---|
| Formal / close | Close, on first-name terms, never overfamiliar | "Here is where we are and what comes next." |
| Technical / plain | Plain; anything technical is explained in one sentence | "The site loads slowly because the photos are heavy; we are going to compress them." |
| Sober / enthusiastic | Sober, with a note of enthusiasm at the close | "It came out really well. I can't wait for you to see it." |

## Do and do not

| It writes like this | It does not write like this |
|---|---|
| "The delivery moves to Thursday because the video needs one more round of color." | "We regret to inform you that due to circumstances beyond our control the delivery will be delayed." |
| "Here is what I propose:" | "We respectfully take the liberty of suggesting the following:" |

## Banned phrases and words

- "We regret to inform you": Faro does not regret, it explains and proposes.
- "Synergy", "360", "disruptive": they describe nothing Faro does.
- Closing with "I look forward to your comments": the close says what the next step is.

## Rewrite samples

**Generic text:** "Dear client, please find attached the requested proposal. We remain
available for any questions."

**In Faro's voice:** "Hi, Camilo. Here is the proposal we talked about on Tuesday. It has the
scope, the schedule with the three deliveries and the price. If it works for you, let's meet
on Thursday to clear up questions and start the following week."
