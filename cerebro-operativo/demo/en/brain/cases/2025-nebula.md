# Nébula Fitness case

> **Source:** told by Marina in the installation interview of 2026-09-01.

## What happened

Nébula Fitness, a chain of three gyms, hired the social media content in 2024. Three months
in, the owner started writing directly to Andrés, at night and by messaging app, demanding
changes for the next day in a tone the team described as "scolding".

## What was done

Marina stepped in. She asked for a call with the owner, listened first, and then put two
things in writing: the channel (a single project email) and the hours (working days, 8 to
6). The team stopped receiving direct messages.

## How it ended

The relationship lasted eight more months with the agreed channel. At renewal, Nébula asked
again to "have someone available" outside working hours; Marina said no and it was not
renewed. All the material was handed over and the open deliverables were closed.

## What was learned

The channel and the hours for reaching the team are agreed in writing at the start of the
project, not after the first problem. And an escalation is taken by a single person, so the
team does not have to decide whether to answer.

## Which value applied

**The team comes first** (value) and **how the team is treated** (non-negotiable), both in
`_config/values-and-negotiables.md`. This case is the one that gave rise to the
non-negotiable.
