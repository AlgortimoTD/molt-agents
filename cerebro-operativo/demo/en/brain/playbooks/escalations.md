## Who answers

<!-- Current since 2026-09-08 (origin: meeting 2026-09-08, minute 27:05) -->

When a client raises their tone, complains outside the agreed channel or writes directly to
someone on the team, **nobody on the team answers in the heat of the moment**. Marina is told,
and she takes over the conversation.

## How to answer

<!-- Current since 2023-03-10 (origin: documents/client-service-manual-2023.md) -->

1. Marina asks for a call; she does not answer by message.
2. On the call she listens first, and then puts in writing the channel and the hours for
   reaching the team (the email that closes the call).
3. If the non-negotiable is broken a second time, what is written in
   `_config/values-and-negotiables.md` applies: the client is let go, on a call.

## Response times

<!-- Current since 2023-03-10 (origin: documents/client-service-manual-2023.md) -->

Every message from a client is answered in less than an hour, by WhatsApp or by email, Monday
to Saturday.
