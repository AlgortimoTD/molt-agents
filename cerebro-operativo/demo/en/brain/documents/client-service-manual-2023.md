# Client service manual

Faro Estudio · version of March 10, 2023

## When a client complains

1. Marina asks for a call; she does not answer by message.
2. On the call she listens first, and then puts in writing the channel and the hours for
   reaching the team (the email that closes the call).
3. If the client skips the agreed channel again, they are let go, on a call.

## Response times

We answer every message from a client in less than an hour, by WhatsApp or by email, Monday
to Saturday.
